Capture

Capture hotkey

Stops Horu reading itself when it overlaps the game. Turn off to show Horu when streaming or sharing your screen.

Minimizing leaves just Horu's icon by the clock: click it to bring Horu back. Its menu can also capture and turn auto-capture on or off.

Auto-capture (the eye button in Read) reads the game by itself whenever new text settles on screen, and shows the dialogue it finds: lines, choices, thought bubbles. Text that stays put (the HUD) or moves with the camera (signs) doesn't set it off. If a game's HUD keeps changing, limit it to parts of the picture: open a line's ⋯ menu and pick "Set auto-read regions".

Study

In Horu: Save keeps a word here with its line and screenshot, for review in the Study tab. With Anki: the word popup adds cards to Anki, and can still save a word in Horu.

Anki

Off by default. For each new dialogue line, the AI picks the words worth learning at your level and adds those not already in your deck (up to 3 per line). Uses one AI task per line and needs a model with tool support.

Cards use a "Horu Sentence" note type: the line with the word in bold, then its reading, meaning, grammar, translation, and a screenshot.

AI

API key

Small local models translate well, but their grammar explanations can be confidently wrong. Horu's own word popups (dictionary form and grammar notes) don't use AI and are reliable.

The AI reads each new dialogue line (and a name tag OCR wasn't sure of) from a picture of it, to catch OCR misreads. One request with an image per line; models that can't read images are skipped. Lines from a text hooker are never checked.

When OCR may have misread a line or a speaker's name, the AI reads it from the screenshot and Horu uses that reading, marked "corrected" (click to undo). Off: Horu asks "Did you mean …?" instead, unless another OCR model read the same thing.

Text hooker

For PC games a text hooker can attach to (most visual novels): exact text, no OCR. Run the hooker (Textractor, LunaHook, ...) and pick the game's text thread there. Clipboard: turn on the hooker's copy-to-clipboard option (in Textractor, the Copy to Clipboard extension); anything Japanese you copy shows up too. WebSocket: a hooker that sends lines to texthooker pages, such as Textractor with the textractor_websocket extension.

Help

Problems

When Horu reads a screen wrongly: a zip with the screenshot and what Horu made of it, to attach to a bug report. A line's ⋯ menu saves the capture that line came from.

Auto-read regions ·

Optional: with regions, auto-capture only notices new text inside them. Cover each box where text you want read appears, with room for the name tag and longer lines. Drag a region to move it, its corners to resize it, or drag on the picture to add one.

No regions: auto-capture watches the whole picture.
